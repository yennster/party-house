Hello,

I'm the developer of **Party House**, a free and open-source iOS/macOS app
(no ads, accounts, purchases, or data collection) that lets a user control the

long-lived access token the user creates; nothing is routed through any service
of mine.

Apple's App Store review (Guideline 5.2.1) has asked for documentary evidence of
authorization to reference Home Assistant. I understand the foundation's brand
policy restricts **commercial use of the logo/marks**, and I want to be clearly
within bounds:

- I use **no** Home Assistant or Open Home Foundation logo anywhere.
- I refer to "Home Assistant" only **descriptively**, to indicate that the app
  connects to a user's own HA server (nominative use), not as the app's name.
- The app carries a non-affiliation/trademark disclaimer in-app and on the
  App Store listing, and it is free/open-source.

Could you provide a short written confirmation that this descriptive use by a
free, open-source companion app is permitted? I'd submit it to Apple as
supporting evidence. Happy to share the repo, screenshots, or the exact wording.
